# Dataset Datasheet | Regime-Aware Options Framework

| Category         | Details |
|------------------|---------|
| **Asset** | VEV (Underlying + Options Chain) |
| **Regimes** | Random Walk (GBM), Mean Reverting (OU) |
| **Data Tiers** | Tier 1 (Empirical), Tier 2 (Synthetic) |
| **Timeframe** | May 10, 2026 – May 29, 2026 (Sprint Duration) |
| **Objective** | Validating uncertainty-aware pricing and regime detection |

### Motivation

**What task does this dataset help to solve?**
This dataset was assembled to support the development of a regime-switching options trading framework. It enables the validation of three critical components: (1) **Regime Classification**: Distinguishing between random walk and mean-reverting dynamics. (2) **Calibration with Uncertainty**: Estimating model parameters with associated standard errors via block bootstrapping. (3) **Fair Value Pricing**: Generating price bands that account for parameter uncertainty to identify market richness or cheapness.

**Who created the dataset and who funded it?**
The Tier 1 empirical data is provided by the IMC Prosperity challenge. The Tier 2 synthetic data is programmatically generated by the internal `src/synthetic.py` module, developed by the student researcher (Hassan) as part of an undergraduate quantitative research initiative.

### Composition

**What do the instances represent?**
* **Tier 1 (Empirical):** Row-wise market snapshots containing bid/ask prices and volumes for the VEV underlying and its associated options across nine strikes (e.g., 5200, 5300).
* **Tier 2 (Synthetic):** Digitally simulated price paths. GBM paths represent the Random Walk regime, while OU paths represent the Mean Reverting regime.

**How many instances of each type are there?**
* **Tier 1:** 3 days of high-frequency trading data (Round 4, Days 1-3) with 1,000,000 timestamps per day.
* **Tier 2:** Generated on-demand for validation; typically consists of 1,000+ paths per regime for statistical coverage testing.

**Is there any missing data?**
Tier 1 contains intermittent gaps in the options chain, particularly for deep out-of-the-money (OTM) strikes. Tier 2 is fully continuous.

### Collection Process

**How was the data acquired?**
Tier 1 was exported from the IMC Prosperity simulation platform via CSV. Tier 2 is generated using the `numpy` and `scipy` libraries, employing a strict seed policy in `src/synthetic.py` to ensure experimental reproducibility across the 20-day sprint.

**Over what time frame was the data collected?**
Tier 1 data corresponds to the historical Round 4 window. Tier 2 data is generated throughout the project sprint window: Sunday, May 10 – Friday, May 29, 2026.

### Preprocessing/Cleaning/Labelling

**Was any preprocessing done?**
Yes. Tier 1 CSVs are parsed to extract mid-prices. The dynamics engine (`src/dynamics.py`) transforms this into log-returns for the Random Walk detector and raw price levels for the Mean Reverting detector. No-look-ahead constraints are strictly enforced during the preprocessing stage for backtesting.

**Was the "raw" data saved?**
The original CSV files (`prices_round_4_day_n.csv`) are preserved in the repository to maintain the integrity of the strict no-look-ahead replay.

### Uses

**What other tasks could the dataset be used for?**
The synthetic generators can be adapted for stress-testing volatility estimators or verifying the convergence properties of various bootstrap methods (e.g., stationary bootstrap vs. block bootstrap).

**Are there tasks for which the dataset should not be used?**
The synthetic data should not be used for absolute directional prediction of real markets, as it assumes idealized stochastic processes that do not account for external macroeconomic shocks or liquidity crises.

### Maintenance

The dataset is maintained by the project lead. The Tier 1 component is frozen as of the completion of Round 4, while the Tier 2 generator is version-controlled within the repository. The framework is designed to be append-only, allowing for a "Trending" regime to be added in Phase A without modifying existing validation records.
