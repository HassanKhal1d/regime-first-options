# Datasheet: Regime-Aware Options Framework

## Motivation

* **For what purpose was the dataset created?**
  The dataset serves two purposes: (1) Tier 1 Empirical data provides a realistic market environment for backtesting and strategy validation on the VEV asset. (2) Tier 2 Synthetic data is created to validate model calibration, pricing intervals, and regime detection against known ground-truth parameters (GBM and OU processes).

* **Who created the dataset?**
  Tier 1 data was provided by the IMC Prosperity challenge environment. Tier 2 data is generated by the internal `src/synthetic.py` module developed for this sprint.

## Composition

* **What do the instances represent?**
  * **Tier 1 (Empirical):** Market snapshots of the VEV asset, including mid-prices and an options chain across nine strikes (e.g., VEV_5200, VEV_5300).
  * **Tier 2 (Synthetic):** Simulated price paths following Geometric Brownian Motion (Random Walk) and Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (Mean Reverting) dynamics.

* **How many instances of each type are there?**
  * Tier 1 includes 3 days of historical price and trade data (Round 4, Days 1-3) with timestamps from 0 to 999,900.
  * Tier 2 is generated on-demand; validation typically uses 1,000+ paths per regime to ensure statistical significance for bootstrap coverage.

* **Is there any missing data?**
  Tier 1 data may contain gaps in illiquid option strikes. Tier 2 is perfectly continuous by design.

* **Does the dataset contain confidential data?**
  No. All data used is either publicly available through the challenge or synthetically generated.

## Collection Process

* **How was the data acquired?**
  * **Tier 1:** Exported from the IMC Prosperity simulation platform (Round 4).
  * **Tier 2:** Programmatically generated using `numpy` and `scipy` within `src/synthetic.py`, utilizing a strict seed policy for reproducibility.

* **Over what time frame was the data collected?**
  Tier 1 covers three distinct trading days. Tier 2 is generated for the 20-day sprint duration (May 10 – May 29, 2026).

## Preprocessing/Cleaning/Labelling

* **Was any preprocessing done?**
  Yes. Tier 1 CSVs are parsed to extract mid-prices and formatted into a unified time-series for the `dynamics.py` detectors. Log-returns are calculated for the GBM regime, while raw price levels are used for the OU (mean-reverting) regime.

* **Was the "raw" data saved?**
  Yes, original CSV files (`prices_round_4_day_n.csv`) are maintained in the repository to ensure no-look-ahead replay integrity.

## Uses

* **What other tasks could the dataset be used for?**
  The synthetic Tier 2 generators can be used to stress-test any volatility-dependent strategy or to train alternative regime classifiers.

* **Are there tasks for which the dataset should not be used?**
  Tier 2 data should not be used to predict real-world VEV market behavior, as it assumes idealized mathematical dynamics (GBM/OU) that may ignore market microstructure.

## Distribution

* **How has the dataset already been distributed?**
  Internal to the project team via the repository.

* **Is it subject to license?**
  Tier 1 is subject to IMC Prosperity terms of use. Tier 2 is proprietary to the project framework.

## Maintenance

* **Who maintains the dataset?**
  The project lead maintains the synthetic generators; the IMC Prosperity platform maintains the empirical source data.
